# Competition Journey

Project: Agro-Smart Hub

---

# Overview

Agro-Smart Hub was developed for the Battle of the Bots Robotics Competition 2.0 under the theme **"Robotics for Industrialization."**

The project aimed to demonstrate how mechatronics and renewable energy could be applied to automate part of the cassava processing workflow, specifically the sieving stage before gari production.

The prototype integrated mechanical systems, embedded control, wireless communication, renewable energy, and an Android-based control interface into a single working system.

---

# Inspiration

The inspiration for Agro-Smart Hub came from my childhood experiences assisting my grandparents in cassava farming and gari production.

Having participated in harvesting, peeling, washing, grinding, drying, and sieving cassava, I became familiar with the manual effort required throughout the process. When preparing for the competition, I reflected on these experiences and identified cassava sieving as a process that could benefit from automation.

This personal connection shaped the direction of the project.

---

# Project Objectives

The primary objectives were:

- Automate cassava sieving.
- Demonstrate subsystem integration.
- Incorporate renewable energy.
- Develop a wireless control system.
- Build the project alongside secondary school students as an educational engineering experience.

---

# Engineering Approach

The project was divided into independent subsystems:

- Renewable Power Station
- Battery Pack
- Slant Conveyor
- Siever
- Flat Conveyor
- Transport Truck
- Android Controller
- Wireless Communication System

Each subsystem was designed, constructed, tested, and verified independently before full system integration.

---

# Team Development

Since the competition was student-based, an important objective was to involve students throughout the engineering process.

Students received instruction in:

- Mechanical engineering fundamentals
- Electrical systems
- Embedded programming
- Engineering documentation
- Testing and debugging
- Technical presentation

This transformed the project into both an engineering and educational initiative.

---

# Technical Challenges

Several significant engineering challenges arose during development.

These included:

- Selecting an appropriate wireless communication method.
- Debugging motor control problems.
- Improving Bluetooth communication reliability.
- Redesigning the truck lifting mechanism.
- Resolving relay wiring faults.
- Managing integration issues between independently developed subsystems.

Each challenge required systematic investigation, testing, and iterative redesign before a satisfactory solution was achieved.

---

# Competition Experience

The completed prototype successfully passed internal testing and the competition's preliminary technical review.

During the competition, the system successfully demonstrated multiple subsystems and advanced through the initial judging stages.

Unfortunately, during a later demonstration, the main battery pack powering the renewable power station failed unexpectedly. Although this prevented completion of the full demonstration sequence, the team continued presenting the engineering concepts, design decisions, and completed work to the judges.

The project finished in 12th place among 38 competing secondary school teams.

---

# Lessons Beyond the Competition

Although the competition result was not the outcome the team had hoped for, the project achieved several important objectives.

It demonstrated the successful integration of mechanical, electrical, embedded, and software systems into a functional prototype. It also provided valuable experience in engineering design, project management, leadership, mentoring, and systematic problem solving.

Perhaps most importantly, the project strengthened my interest in mechatronics engineering and reinforced my desire to pursue advanced study and research in intelligent mechanical systems.

---

# Legacy

Agro-Smart Hub represents more than a competition entry.

It reflects a journey of learning, teaching, perseverance, and engineering creativity. The knowledge gained throughout the project continues to influence my approach to engineering design and serves as a strong foundation for future research and development in mechatronics engineering.
