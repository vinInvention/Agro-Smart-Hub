# User Manual

Project: Agro-Smart Hub

---

# Purpose

This manual provides instructions for the safe setup, operation, and shutdown of the Agro-Smart Hub prototype.

---

# System Overview

The Agro-Smart Hub is a prototype mechatronic system developed to automate the cassava sieving stage of gari production. It consists of:

- Renewable Power Station
- Slant Conveyor
- Siever
- Flat Conveyor
- Transport Truck
- Android Controller

The system is controlled wirelessly through an Android application.

---

# Safety Precautions

Before operating the system:

- Ensure all electrical connections are secure.
- Check that the battery pack is adequately charged.
- Keep hands away from moving mechanisms during operation.
- Do not overload the conveyors or truck.
- Switch off the system before performing maintenance.

---

# System Startup

## Step 1

Place the Agro-Smart Hub on a stable, level surface.

---

## Step 2

Power on the renewable power station.

---

## Step 3

Power on the truck and hub controllers.

---

## Step 4

Open the Android controller application.

---

## Step 5

Connect the Android application to the truck via Bluetooth.

---

## Step 6

Verify that communication has been established before issuing commands.

---

# Operating the System

The system should be operated in the following sequence:

1. Start the slant conveyor.
2. Start the siever.
3. Start the flat conveyor.
4. Load cassava onto the slant conveyor.
5. Operate the truck as required.
6. Activate the truck lift mechanism to unload processed cassava.

---

# System Shutdown

1. Stop the truck.
2. Stop the flat conveyor.
3. Stop the siever.
4. Stop the slant conveyor.
5. Disconnect the Android application.
6. Switch off the controllers.
7. Switch off the renewable power station.

---

# Troubleshooting

| Problem                    | Possible Cause                         | Suggested Action                                |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| No power                   | Battery discharged or switch off       | Recharge battery and verify power connections   |
| Bluetooth will not connect | Bluetooth module not powered or paired | Verify power, pairing, and connection settings  |
| Truck does not move        | Motor driver or wiring issue           | Check motor driver, wiring, and battery voltage |
| Conveyor does not operate  | Relay or motor issue                   | Inspect relay outputs and motor connections     |
| Commands not executed      | Communication interruption             | Restart the Bluetooth connection and retry      |

---

# Maintenance Before Use

Before each demonstration or operation:

- Inspect wiring for loose connections.
- Verify battery charge.
- Confirm Bluetooth communication.
- Check that all moving parts rotate freely.
- Ensure conveyors are properly aligned.

---

# Intended Use

The Agro-Smart Hub is an educational engineering prototype designed for demonstrations, research, and learning. It is not intended for commercial or industrial cassava processing.
