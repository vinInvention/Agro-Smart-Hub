# Agro-Smart Hub

## Project Overview

Agro-Smart Hub is a solar-powered mechatronic system designed to automate selected stages of cassava processing. The project was developed for the Battle of the Bots Robotics Competition 2.0 under the theme **"Robotics for Industrialization."**

The system combines mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, embedded systems, renewable energy, and mobile application development into a single integrated solution. It demonstrates how robotics can improve agricultural processing by reducing manual labour, increasing efficiency, and promoting sustainable energy use.

Rather than attempting to automate the entire cassava processing chain, the project focuses on the post-grinding stage of processing. Specifically, it automates the sieving of cassava mash, transports the sieved material using conveyor systems, and delivers it to a designated frying area using a mobile robotic truck.

---

## Problem Statement

Traditional cassava processing involves several labour-intensive operations. One of these is the sieving stage, where fine cassava particles are manually separated from coarse particles before frying. This process is repetitive, physically demanding, and time-consuming, limiting productivity for small-scale processors.

The Agro-Smart Hub project was developed to demonstrate how mechatronic engineering principles can be applied to automate this stage while integrating renewable energy and wireless control technologies.

---

## Project Objectives

The project aimed to:

- Design and construct an automated cassava sieving system.
- Develop a renewable solar-powered electrical system.
- Build a mobile robotic truck for material transportation.
- Develop an Android application for wireless system control.
- Integrate mechanical, electrical, and embedded subsystems into a single mechatronic system.
- Demonstrate industrial automation concepts suitable for agricultural applications.
- Provide students with practical experience in multidisciplinary engineering design.

---

## Major Subsystems

The complete Agro-Smart Hub consists of the following subsystems:

1. Renewable solar power station
2. Rechargeable battery pack
3. Slant conveyor
4. Oscillating cassava sieving mechanism
5. Flat conveyor
6. Mobile robotic transport truck
7. Android control application
8. Wireless communication system
9. Embedded control system

## Educational Impact

The project was developed as a student-centered engineering project over approximately six months. Throughout the development process, students participated in the design, construction, testing, debugging, and presentation of the system while learning fundamental concepts in mechanical engineering, electrical systems, embedded programming, robotics, and engineering design.

---

## Competition Outcome

A fully functional prototype was successfully designed, constructed, integrated, and demonstrated during the Battle of the Bots Robotics Competition 2.0.

Although the prototype experienced an unexpected battery pack failure during the competition, the project successfully demonstrated the feasibility of integrating renewable energy, embedded control, wireless communication, and mechanical automation into a unified agricultural mechatronic system. The experience provided valuable engineering lessons in system integration, testing, reliability, and project execution.
