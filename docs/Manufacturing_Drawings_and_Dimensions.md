# Manufacturing Drawings and Dimensions

Project: Agro-Smart Hub

---

# Purpose

This document provides an index of the manufacturing drawings and dimensional information developed for the Agro-Smart Hub prototype.

The prototype was fabricated primarily from plywood, ceiling board, PVC pipes, steel shafts, bearings, and custom-built mechanisms. Due to the iterative nature of the prototype development, not all component dimensions were formally documented during fabrication.

The drawings referenced below illustrate the construction and layout of the major subsystems.

# Notes

The Agro-Smart Hub was developed as an iterative engineering prototype. During fabrication, component dimensions were adjusted as required to achieve proper alignment, motion, and subsystem integration. Where available, measured dimensions may be added in future revisions.
